Lumi · three SVG takes

Quick exploration, 2026-09-24, parked until there is time for art. Hand-written SVG in the Sage & Gilt palette, six expressions each, with an idle bob, blink and twinkle (off under reduced motion). A full set is a few KB and animates without extra images. The painted GPT busts in assets/lumi_current_design/ stay as the fallback.

A. Star sprite

Lumi is the spirit of her hairpin: the Lumi Star with a face. The most ownable and the easiest to animate; it doubles as the mark. Loses the "person" feel.

B. Paper portrait

Lumi as a few flat, layered shapes: hair, face, collar, star pin. A calm modern avatar that stays readable at 54 px. Closest to the GPT busts, but lighter.

C. Constellation

Portrait B traced as a gold constellation, drawing itself in; the nodes are stars. Ties Lumi to the Star Atlas map. The least expressive face.
