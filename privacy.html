Life Balance Index is a local-first personal wellbeing tool. It has no account system, no server, and no database. This statement explains exactly what happens to your data.

What data the app holds

When you use the app you may enter demographic details, income and financial habits, physical-activity and sleep data, and answers to validated psychological screeners (well-being, stress, loneliness, social network, self-efficacy, grit, and others). All of this is assessment data about you.

Where it is stored

  • Everything is stored only in your browser's localStorage, on your own device. It is never transmitted to us or to any third-party server, because the app has no backend to send it to.
  • There is no analytics, no tracking, and no telemetry. We do not collect usage data, crash reports, or identifiers.

When data leaves your device

Your data only leaves your device when you deliberately export or share it:

  • Export backup produces a JSON file that you save wherever you choose. It contains your full local data. Keep it somewhere you trust.
  • Comparison Codes (the peer-comparison feature) encode only your display name and the eight 0–100 aspect scores — never your raw questionnaire answers, income, health details, age, level, or points. You choose who to share a code with.

Connected apps

Two other apps of ours — Midori (a ledger) and Runaway (a run tracker) — are served from this same address. That means they can hand data to this app through your browser's own storage, without any network request and without an account. Nothing is sent anywhere; one page on your device reads what another page on your device wrote.

  • It is off until you switch it on, per app, on the Profile & Data page — and separately in the source app, which has its own switch controlling whether it shares at all. Both ends must agree.
  • Only aggregates cross over: monthly income and expenses from Midori; days run and total minutes from Runaway. Never a transaction, never a merchant, never a run note, never a name.
  • What arrives is pre-filled into the weekly review for you to confirm or correct, never applied to a score behind your back.
  • Switching a connection off stops the reading immediately. It is a setting in this app, so it survives an erase and is not part of any backup you export.

Third parties

None. All assets — including fonts — are served from the app's own origin. The app makes no third-party network requests at all.

Deleting your data

  • Reset Data in the app clears your local save.
  • Clearing your browser's site data for this page removes everything.
  • Because nothing is stored off your device, deletion is complete and immediate — there are no server copies to request removal of.

Thailand PDPA note

Under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), a "data controller" is an entity that collects, uses, or discloses personal data. Life Balance Index does not collect your assessment data: it is processed locally, on your device, under your sole control, and is never received by the developer. You remain in control of any backup file or Comparison Code you choose to export or share.

Not medical advice

The app administers screening instruments (e.g. WHO-5, ST-5) for self-insight. It is not a diagnosis and not a substitute for professional care. If your results suggest distress, please contact a professional — in Thailand, the Department of Mental Health hotline is 1323 (free, 24 hours).
